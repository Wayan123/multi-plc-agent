# CP1H traffic-light LD example request

Create an original offline educational example for Omron **CP1H-X40DT-D**, device CP1H CPU X, in section/rung `program.json` format and its derived mnemonic `program.il`. Use the mnemonic subset supported by `contoh/omron/offline.py`; include `signals.csv` and an English README. Do not download, go online, force, change hardware, or claim the program is safety certified.

Use 14 separate outputs: north red/yellow/green 100.00–100.02; south 100.03–100.05; east 100.06,100.07,101.00; west 101.01–101.03; pedestrian red/green 101.04–101.05. North–south and east–west remain paired in the phase sequence, not four independent green phases. Explain sinking transistor outputs, terminal limits and the need for drivers when ratings are insufficient. Each physical output has exactly one writer.

Without buttons, automatically repeat this sequence: startup with all vehicles red and pedestrians red for 2 s; north-south green 10 s, yellow 3 s; all red 2 s; east-west green 10 s, yellow 3 s; all red 2 s; pedestrians green while all vehicles are red for 5 s; all red 2 s; repeat from north-south green. No turns are permitted while pedestrians are green. Times are illustrative only, not a road safety determination. On the first scan initialize the stage without depending on retained values. Use BCD timers in 0.1 s units and explain the effect of scan order.

Include independent evaluator scenarios for retained stage values at startup and timing boundaries; every transition through one complete cycle and its repetition; and exclusivity of each color and pedestrian command. Distinguish state injection in the evaluator from PLC forcing. Explain that without independent checks and feedback, output failures, power loss or PLC stop do not guarantee all red; public roads require an independent safety controller and authorized design and validation. Do not describe flashing as a safe state. Include primary references to manuals W450 and W451 and explain that scenarios are not native/hardware evidence.
